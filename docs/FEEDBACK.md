## How to run a session

Pick one or two people outside the team. Give each one a role and the synthetic Campus Events App, and say nothing about how the screens work. Watch, and write down where they hesitate. A session takes about 15 minutes.

| Role | What they are asked to do |
| --- | --- |
| Lead | Connect the sources, run the analysis, review the statements, resolve the conflict, approve |
| Departing member | Answer the questions about things that are not written down anywhere |
| Incoming member | Read the handover, find who to ask about hosting, say what is still unclear, confirm receipt |

## Questions to ask afterwards

1. What did you think a "verified" label meant before you clicked on it? Was it right?
2. Was there a point where you did not know what to do next? Where?
3. Did the questions for the departing member feel like the right questions?
4. As the incoming member, would you trust this enough to start work from it? What is missing?
5. Was anything shown that you did not expect to see, or hidden that you expected to see?
